About The Book

There Is an I in Win

Through powerful storytelling, personal insights, and faith-based reflection, author Joey Crum challenges the long-held belief that individual ambition must take a back seat to collective harmony.

Drawing from his own life experiences ranging from childhood struggles and leadership lessons to moments of failure and personal transformation, Crum makes the case for embracing the misunderstood "I" in every pursuit.
