Meet Joey Crum

Joey Crum is a visionary leader, author, and advocate for personal empowerment. In There Is An “I” In Win, he challenges the age-old notion that individualism must be sacrificed for team success. Joey shows how embracing the “I” initiative, innovation, and integrity transforms not only personal growth but team impact.
